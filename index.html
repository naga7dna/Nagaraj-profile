// about

Where genome biology meets
distributed systems engineering.

I bridge two worlds: wet-lab genomics and production-grade software. My day-to-day is equal parts variant calling, pipeline automation, and full-stack tooling for clinical teams.

As a Clinical Bioinformatician I at 4baseCare, I design and operate end-to-end NGS pipelines for somatic DNA, germline, and RNA-sequencing data. I've personally overseen 1,700+ clinical samples across 80+ batches — running QC on every pipeline run, detecting SNVs, indels, CNVs, and gene fusions, and turning raw FASTQs into clinically actionable reports.

Beyond analysis, I build the software that makes the analysis scale. I've engineered distributed pipeline automation using Python (Flask), Celery and Redis for multi-node job scheduling, containerised Ensembl VEP for high-throughput variant annotation, and shipped a React + Express.js + MySQL full-stack application for CNV visualisation with box plots and heat maps.

My foundation is in genomics (M.Sc., Madurai Kamaraj University) and biochemistry (B.Sc., gold medalist), complemented by a diploma in Python programming. I'm equally comfortable at a sequencer, a terminal, or a whiteboard — and I think the most interesting problems in clinical genomics are the ones that need all three.
